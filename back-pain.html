Back pain背痛

Back pain is one of the most common reasons people seek acupuncture. It may be a dull ache after a long day, stiffness first thing in the morning, a sharp pain after lifting something heavy, or pain that comes and goes with stress or the weather. Most back pain is not caused by one single serious problem, but pain that is severe, persistent, or comes with other symptoms deserves attention. Acupuncture is widely used to help ease back pain and support recovery — many people report feeling looser and more comfortable after treatment.背痛是人們尋求針灸最常見的原因之一。可能是忙了一天後的隱隱痠痛、早上起床時的僵硬、提重物後的劇痛，或是隨著壓力、天氣時好時壞的疼痛。大多數背痛並非由單一嚴重問題引起，但如果疼痛嚴重、持續不退，或伴隨其他症狀，就值得留意。針灸廣泛用於幫助緩解背痛、支持恢復——不少人反映治療後感覺比較鬆、比較舒服。

How TCM looks at back pain中醫怎麼看背痛

In TCM, back pain is not treated by the pain alone — a practitioner looks at the whole picture: when it hurts, what makes it better or worse, and what else is going on in the body. A few patterns often described in TCM texts:在中醫看來，背痛不能只看疼痛本身——醫師會看整體：什麼時候痛、什麼讓它好轉或加重、身體還有什麼其他狀況。以下是中醫文獻中常提到的幾種情況：

  • Cold and Damp — an aching, heavy pain that is worse in cold or damp weather, with stiffness that eases with warmth and gentle movement.寒濕——痠痛沉重，寒冷潮濕天氣加重，遇暖和輕微活動後緩解，伴隨僵硬。
  • Kidney deficiency — a chronic, aching lower back, worse when tired or overworked, sometimes with weak knees or cold feet. In TCM the lower back is linked to the Kidney system, which is said to govern the bones and the lower back.腎虛——慢性的腰部痠痛，疲勞或勞累後加重，有時伴隨膝軟、腳冷。中醫認為腰為腎之府，腰部與腎的系統相關。
  • Blood stasis — a fixed, stabbing pain you can point to with one finger, often after an injury or strain, and often worse at night.血瘀——固定、刺痛，可以用一根手指指出位置，常在受傷或拉傷後出現，夜間往往加重。
  • Liver Qi stagnation — pain that moves around or flares up with stress and frustration, sometimes with a tight chest or frequent sighing.肝氣鬱結——疼痛位置不固定，或隨壓力、煩躁而發作，有時伴隨胸悶、常嘆氣。

These are general descriptions from TCM theory, not a diagnosis. Your own pattern is identified in person — through conversation, tongue inspection and pulse — and it can change over time.以上只是中醫理論的一般描述，不是診斷。個人的證型需經面診——透過問診、看舌苔與把脈——才能判斷，而且證型會隨時間變化。

Gentle self-care for your back背部的日常調養

These are general habits, not a prescription. Move gently and stop any activity that sharply increases pain:以下是一般的生活習慣，不是處方。動作要輕柔，任何讓疼痛明顯加重的活動都要停下來：

1. Keep moving, gently1. 保持輕柔活動

Walking and gentle stretching usually help more than staying in bed. Avoid bed rest beyond a day or two, and if you sit a lot, get up and move at least once an hour.散步和輕柔伸展通常比臥床更有幫助。臥床休息不要超過一兩天；如果久坐，至少每小時起來活動一次。

2. Keep the lower back warm2. 腰部注意保暖

A warm compress, warm clothing, or a warm bath can ease stiffness. Avoid cold drafts, sitting on cold surfaces, and sudden chilling after sweating.熱敷、穿暖一點或泡個熱水澡，都有助緩解僵硬。避免冷風直吹、坐在冰冷的地面上，以及出汗後突然受涼。

3. Lift and sleep with care3. 搬抬與睡姿要留意

Lift with your legs, not your back, and keep loads close to your body. Sleep on your side with a pillow between your knees, or on your back with a pillow under your knees.搬東西時用腿發力，不要用腰；重物貼近身體。睡覺時側臥、兩膝間夾個枕頭，或仰臥、膝下墊個枕頭。

4. Acupressure for back pain4. 背痛穴位按壓

Press gently — enough to feel a mild ache or warmth, never sharp pain. Stop if pain worsens.輕柔按壓——以感覺輕微痠脹或溫熱為度，不要按到劇痛。如果疼痛加重就停下來。

  • Weizhong 委中 (BL40) — in the centre of the crease at the back of the knee. Sit with the knee bent and press 1–2 minutes on each side.委中 (BL40)——膝蓋後方橫紋正中央。坐著屈膝，每側按 1 至 2 分鐘。
  • Shenshu 腎俞 (BL23) — on the lower back, about two finger-widths either side of the spine at waist level. Rub warmly with your palms, or press gently for 1–2 minutes; a tennis ball against a wall can help you reach it.腎俞 (BL23)——腰部、脊柱兩側約兩指寬處。用溫熱的手掌揉搓，或輕按 1 至 2 分鐘；用網球頂著牆也能幫助按到。
  • Hegu 合谷 (LI4) — in the web between the thumb and index finger. Press 1–2 minutes on each side; a general point often used for pain. Avoid strong stimulation in pregnancy.合谷 (LI4)——拇指與食指之間的虎口處。每側按 1 至 2 分鐘，是常用於止痛的穴位。懷孕期間請避免強刺激。

When to seek prompt medical care什麼情況要盡快看醫生

Most back pain settles with time and gentle care, but some signs need prompt medical assessment. Please see your family doctor or urgent care promptly if you notice any of these:大多數背痛會隨著時間和細心調養慢慢好轉，但有些徵兆需要盡快做醫療評估。如果出現以下任何情況，請盡快看家庭醫生或急診：

  • Numbness, tingling or weakness in the legs or feet腿或腳麻木、刺痛或無力
  • Loss of bladder or bowel control大小便失禁
  • Fever with back pain, or pain after a fall or accident背痛伴隨發燒，或跌倒、意外後出現疼痛
  • Unexplained weight loss, or a history of cancer with new back pain不明原因的體重減輕，或有癌症病史又新出現背痛
  • Pain that does not improve after a few weeks, or pain that keeps getting worse幾週後仍無改善，或疼痛持續加重
