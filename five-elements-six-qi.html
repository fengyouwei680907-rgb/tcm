Seasonal self-care ideas

Spring · Gentle movement

Keep a regular sleep routine, gradually increase outdoor activity and avoid overexertion.

Summer · Hydration and heat awareness

Stay hydrated, take heat precautions and adapt activity to temperature and personal tolerance.

Autumn · Protect respiratory comfort

Maintain indoor comfort, avoid smoke and dryness, and follow recommended vaccinations.

Winter · Warmth and steady routines

Dress for conditions, reduce fall risks on icy surfaces and maintain gentle movement.
