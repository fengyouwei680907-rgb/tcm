Sleep problems & insomnia失眠與睡眠困擾

Sleep is when the body restores itself — yet for many people it is the hardest part of the day. Trouble falling asleep, waking in the night, waking too early, vivid dreams, or waking up still tired: sleep problems take many forms. An occasional poor night is common. When poor sleep lasts for weeks and starts to affect your daytime energy, mood, concentration or work, it is worth paying attention to. Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) has thought carefully about sleep for centuries, and acupuncture is one of the approaches many people turn to for support.睡眠是身體恢復的時間——但對很多人來說，卻是一天中最難的部分。難以入睡、半夜醒來、太早醒、夢多、醒來仍覺得累：睡眠問題有很多種樣子。偶爾睡不好很常見；如果持續數週，開始影響白天的精神、情緒、專注力或工作，就值得留意。中醫對睡眠的認識已有上千年歷史，針灸也是不少人尋求幫助的方式之一。

How TCM looks at sleep中醫怎麼看睡眠

In TCM, “insomnia” is not one single thing. A practitioner asks how sleep is disturbed, because different patterns point in different directions. A few patterns often described in TCM texts:在中醫看來，「失眠」不是單一的問題。醫師會問睡眠是「怎麼」受到干擾的，因為不同的表現指向不同的證型。以下是中醫文獻中常提到的幾種情況：

  • Difficulty falling asleep, with a racing mind — often discussed as the Heart and mind not being settled, sometimes linked with Blood not nourishing the Heart. Vivid dreams, palpitations and restlessness may come with it.難以入睡、思緒停不下來——常與心神不寧有關，有時和血不養心相關；可能伴隨多夢、心悸、煩躁。
  • Waking around 1–3 a.m. with a busy, circling mind — often discussed as Liver Qi not flowing smoothly, with irritability or tension during the day.凌晨一至三點醒來、腦子轉個不停——常與肝氣不暢有關，白天可能伴隨煩躁、緊張。
  • Restless sleep with warmth — hot palms and soles, night sweats, dry mouth, a mind that will not switch off. In TCM this is often described as Yin deficiency with “empty heat” rising.睡不安穩又覺得熱——手足心熱、盜汗、口乾、腦子關不掉。中醫常以陰虛、虛熱上擾來描述。
  • Waking unrefreshed, heavy and foggy — hard to get up, a heavy head, lots of phlegm or mucus. This is often described as Dampness weighing the body down.醒來仍覺得累、頭重、昏沉——起床困難、頭重、痰多。中醫常以痰濕困重來描述。

These are general descriptions from TCM theory, not a diagnosis. Your own pattern is identified in person — through conversation, tongue inspection and pulse — and it can change over time.以上只是中醫理論的一般描述，不是診斷。個人的證型需經面診——透過問診、看舌苔與把脈——才能判斷，而且證型會隨時間變化。

Gentle self-care for sleep幫助睡眠的日常調養

These are general habits, not a prescription. Try one or two at a time:以下是一般的生活習慣，不是處方。一次試一兩項就好：

1. Keep regular hours1. 作息規律

Go to bed and get up at about the same time every day, even on weekends. In TCM, the hours before midnight are considered the most restorative — being in bed by 11 p.m. is a classic recommendation.每天大約同一時間睡覺和起床，即使週末也一樣。中醫認為午夜之前的睡眠最具恢復作用——晚上 11 點前上床是經典的建議。

2. Wind down in the evening2. 晚上慢慢放鬆

Dim the lights and put screens away for the last hour before bed. A warm foot soak, a warm shower, or a few minutes of slow breathing can help the body shift into rest.睡前一小時調暗燈光、放下手機螢幕。溫水泡腳、洗個熱水澡，或幾分鐘慢呼吸，都有助身體轉入休息狀態。

3. Food and drink3. 飲食注意

Avoid caffeine after noon, keep supper light and not too late, and be cautious with alcohol near bedtime — it may help you fall asleep but often fragments sleep later in the night.中午後避免咖啡因，晚餐吃得清淡、不要太晚；睡前飲酒要謹慎——它可能幫助入睡，但常會讓後半夜的睡眠變得破碎。

4. Acupressure before bed4. 睡前穴位按壓

Press gently — enough to feel a mild ache or warmth, never pain. Stop if it feels uncomfortable.輕柔按壓——以感覺輕微痠脹或溫熱為度，不要按到痛。如果不舒服就停下來。

  • Shenmen 神門 (HT7) — on the wrist crease, on the little-finger side, in the hollow just beside the tendon. Press 2 minutes on each wrist. A classic point for calming the mind.神門 (HT7)——手腕橫紋上、小指一側，肌腱旁的凹陷處。每隻手腕按 2 分鐘，是安神的經典穴位。
  • Anmian 安眠 — behind the ear, in the hollow below the bony bump behind the earlobe. Press gently 1–2 minutes on each side.安眠——耳後、耳垂後方骨頭凸起下方的凹陷處。每側輕按 1 至 2 分鐘。
  • Sanyinjiao 三陰交 (SP6) — four finger-widths above the inner ankle bone, just behind the shin bone. Press 2 minutes on each side. Avoid strong stimulation in pregnancy.三陰交 (SP6)——內踝骨上方四指寬、脛骨後緣處。每側按 2 分鐘。懷孕期間請避免強刺激。

When to seek prompt medical care什麼情況要盡快看醫生

Acupuncture does not replace medical assessment. Please see your family doctor promptly if you notice any of these:針灸不能替代醫療評估。如果出現以下任何情況，請盡快看家庭醫生：

  • Sleep problems lasting more than a few weeks that impair daytime functioning睡眠問題持續數週以上，影響白天生活
  • Loud snoring with pauses in breathing, gasping, or waking with a choking feeling (possible sleep apnea)打鼾聲大、呼吸暫停、喘氣，或醒來時有窒息感（可能是睡眠呼吸中止）
  • Falling asleep while driving, at work, or in other unsafe situations開車、工作或在其他不安全的情況下打瞌睡
  • New severe morning headaches, chest pain, shortness of breath, or leg swelling新出現的嚴重晨間頭痛、胸痛、呼吸困難或腿腫
  • Very low mood, or thoughts of harming yourself — please reach out right away. In Canada you can call or text 988, or dial 911 in an emergency.情緒非常低落，或有傷害自己的念頭——請立即求助。在加拿大可撥打或傳訊 988，緊急情況請撥 911。
