Fertility support — trying to conceive助孕支持 — 備孕

Trying to conceive can be a hopeful time — and also a difficult one, especially when months pass without success. Many people come to us feeling tired, stressed, or unsure what else to try. Acupuncture and TCM are often used alongside conventional fertility care to support overall balance, regular cycles and wellbeing during this time. We do not promise pregnancy: every person’s situation is different, and care is always planned individually after an in-person assessment.備孕是一段充滿希望的時間——也可能是一段不容易的時間，尤其是當幾個月過去，仍未如願。很多人來找我們時，感到疲倦、壓力大，或不知道還能做什麼。針灸與中醫常與常規生育醫療配合使用，以支持整體平衡、月經規律與備孕期間的身心健康。我們不承諾懷孕：每個人的情況都不同，治療方案一定經面診後個別制定。

How TCM looks at fertility中醫怎麼看生育

In TCM, fertility is understood as part of the whole body’s balance, not as one isolated function. A practitioner looks at the entire picture — your cycle, sleep, digestion, mood, tongue and pulse — before deciding on a plan. A few ideas often discussed in TCM texts:在中醫看來，生育是整體平衡的一部分，不是單一的功能。醫師會看完整情況——月經週期、睡眠、消化、情緒、舌苔與脈象——再決定方案。以下是中醫文獻中常提到的幾個角度：

  • Kidney essence — in TCM, reproductive vitality is said to be stored in the Kidney system. Supporting Kidney energy is a common focus of fertility care.腎精——中醫認為生殖的精華藏於腎，調理腎氣是助孕調理中常見的重點。
  • Blood — Blood is said to nourish the uterus and support a regular cycle. Very light or scanty periods, pale complexion, or dizziness are often discussed in this light.血——中醫認為血能濡養子宮、支持月經規律。月經量很少、面色蒼白、頭暈，常從這個角度來討論。
  • Liver Qi — stress and emotional tension are said to disturb the smooth flow of Qi and the cycle. Irregular periods, breast tenderness before periods, or mood swings are often discussed here.肝氣——中醫認為壓力與情緒緊張會影響氣的順暢流動，進而影響月經。月經不規律、經前乳房脹痛、情緒波動，常從這個角度來討論。
  • Dampness and Phlegm — heaviness, weight gain around the middle, and sticky or heavy menstrual flow are often discussed alongside conditions such as PCOS in TCM literature.痰濕——身體沉重、腰腹發胖、經血黏稠，在中醫文獻中常與多囊性卵巢症候群（PCOS）等情況一併討論。

These are general descriptions from TCM theory, not a diagnosis. Fertility is also a shared journey — a partner’s health matters too, and a fertility workup looks at both partners.以上只是中醫理論的一般描述，不是診斷。生育也是兩個人的事——伴侶的健康同樣重要，生育檢查會看夫妻雙方。

What a visit or assessment involves看診或評估是怎麼進行的

A good first step is the free 10-question quick check on our main page: it gives a first-glance pattern from your answers, right on the page. The full 40-question professional assessment goes further — your answers and tongue photo are reviewed by a registered practitioner, who prepares a personal analysis (pattern, herbal guidance and self-care priorities) and emails it to you.一個好的起點是主頁上的免費 10 題快速自測：根據您的答案，即時在頁面顯示初步證型。完整 40 題專業評估則更進一步——您的答案與舌苔照片由註冊醫師審閱，醫師會準備個人化分析（證型、方劑指導與自我調養重點），再以電郵寄給您。

An in-person first visit lasts about an hour: a conversation about your health history and cycles, tongue and pulse examination, and then an honest discussion of what TCM may and may not offer in your situation. If you are already seeing a fertility doctor, please tell both practitioners what the other is doing — especially about herbs and medicines, so nothing interacts. Treatment is individualized; how often you come and for how long varies from person to person.線下初診約一小時：聊聊您的健康史與月經週期、看舌苔、把脈，然後誠實地討論中醫在您的情況下能做什麼、不能做什麼。如果您已經在看生育專科醫生，請讓雙方醫師都知道對方在做什麼——尤其是草藥與藥物，以免相互作用。治療是個別化的；來的頻率與療程長短因人而異。

Gentle self-care while trying to conceive備孕期間的日常調養

These are general habits, not a prescription, and they support overall health rather than treating fertility directly:以下是一般的日常習慣，不是處方；它們支持整體健康，而不是直接治療不孕：

1. Eat regularly and warmly1. 定時吃溫熱的食物

Regular, warm, cooked meals — and do not skip breakfast. Moderate caffeine and alcohol. In TCM, Blood is made from food, so steady nourishment matters.定時吃溫熱、煮熟的食物，不要不吃早餐。咖啡因與酒精要適量。中醫認為血由飲食化生，穩定的營養很重要。

2. Protect your sleep and rhythm2. 保護睡眠與生活節律

A regular daily rhythm supports the cycle. Being in bed by 11 p.m. when possible is a classic TCM recommendation.規律的作息有助月經週期。晚上 11 點前上床，是中醫經典的建議。

3. Move gently, manage stress3. 輕柔活動、紓解壓力

Daily gentle movement — walking, stretching, tai chi — and avoid exhausting workouts. Stress affects the cycle, so talking, journaling, or counselling genuinely counts as part of care.每天輕柔活動——散步、伸展、太極，避免過度劇烈的運動。壓力會影響月經週期，傾訴、寫日記或尋求諮詢，確實是調理的一部分。

4. Track your cycle4. 記錄月經週期

Note your period dates, cycle length and any ovulation signs, and bring the notes to your visit — they help the practitioner understand your pattern.記錄月經日期、週期長短與排卵徵兆，看診時帶來——這些有助醫師了解您的證型。

5. Acupressure for general wellbeing5. 穴位按壓——一般保健

Press gently — enough to feel a mild ache or warmth, never pain. Stop if it feels uncomfortable. These points are general self-care, not a fertility treatment on their own.輕柔按壓——以感覺輕微痠脹或溫熱為度，不要按到痛。如果不舒服就停下來。這些穴位屬於一般保健，本身不是助孕治療。

  • Guanyuan 關元 (CV4) — four finger-widths below the navel. Rest a warm palm here, or press gently for 2–3 minutes.關元 (CV4)——肚臍下方四指寬處。用溫熱的手掌敷著，或輕按 2 至 3 分鐘。
  • Zusanli 足三里 (ST36) — below the knee, one finger-width to the outer side of the shin bone. Press firmly 2–3 minutes on each leg.足三里 (ST36)——膝蓋下方、脛骨外側一指寬處。每條腿用力按 2 至 3 分鐘。
  • Sanyinjiao 三陰交 (SP6) — four finger-widths above the inner ankle bone, just behind the shin bone. Press 2 minutes on each side. Once you are pregnant, avoid strong stimulation of Sanyinjiao (and of Hegu 合谷 LI4), and always tell your practitioner you are pregnant so care can be adjusted.三陰交 (SP6)——內踝骨上方四指寬、脛骨後緣處。每側按 2 分鐘。一旦懷孕，請避免強刺激三陰交（以及合谷 LI4），並務必告訴醫師您已懷孕，以便調整調理方式。

When to seek medical care什麼情況要看醫生

TCM support works best alongside proper medical care. Please see your family doctor or a fertility specialist promptly if you notice any of these:中醫調理最好與正規醫療配合。如果出現以下任何情況，請盡快看家庭醫生或生育專科醫生：

  • Irregular or absent periods, or very painful periods月經不規律或停經，或經痛非常嚴重
  • Severe pelvic pain, heavy bleeding, bleeding between periods, fever, or unusual discharge嚴重的骨盆疼痛、經血過多、非經期出血、發燒或異常分泌物
  • Known conditions such as PCOS or endometriosis已知有多囊性卵巢症候群（PCOS）或子宮內膜異位症等情況
  • If you are over 35 and have been trying for more than 6 months, or over 40, seek medical advice sooner35 歲以上、嘗試超過 6 個月仍未懷孕，或 40 歲以上，請提早尋求醫療建議
  • If your partner has a known fertility issue伴侶已知有生育方面的問題
