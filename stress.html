Stress & anxiety壓力與焦慮

Stress is part of life — deadlines, family, money, health worries. A little stress can sharpen focus, but when it stays high for weeks, it wears the body down: tight shoulders, headaches, poor sleep, upset digestion, low mood, or feeling on edge all the time. Many people come to us when stress has started to show up in the body. Acupuncture is often used to help ease that tension and support rest — many people report feeling calmer and sleeping better after treatment.壓力是生活的一部分——工作期限、家庭、金錢、健康擔憂。適度的壓力可以讓人專注，但如果壓力持續數週居高不下，會慢慢拖垮身體：肩頸緊繃、頭痛、睡不好、腸胃不適、情緒低落，或整天覺得緊張不安。很多人都是在壓力開始表現在身體上時來找我們。針灸常用於幫助緩解這種緊張、支持休息——不少人反映治療後感覺比較平靜、睡得比較好。

How TCM looks at stress中醫怎麼看壓力

In TCM, stress is most often described as Liver Qi stagnation — Qi that should flow smoothly gets stuck under emotional pressure. But stress rarely affects one system alone. A few patterns often described in TCM texts:在中醫看來，壓力最常以肝氣鬱結來描述——本應順暢流動的氣，在情緒壓力下卡住了。但壓力很少只影響一個系統。以下是中醫文獻中常提到的幾種情況：

  • Liver Qi stagnation — irritability, frequent sighing, a tight chest, tension headaches, or pain that moves around. Symptoms characteristically flare when you are stressed and ease when you relax.肝氣鬱結——煩躁、常嘆氣、胸悶、緊張性頭痛，或疼痛位置游走不定。症狀通常在壓力大時加重、放鬆時緩解。
  • Restless mind at night — a racing mind, vivid dreams, difficulty falling asleep. In TCM this is often discussed as the Heart and mind not being settled — sometimes linked with Blood or Yin not nourishing the Heart.夜裡腦子停不下來——思緒奔騰、多夢、難以入睡。中醫常以心神不寧來描述，有時與血或陰不足以養心有關。
  • Worry and overthinking — in TCM, worry is said to “knot” the Spleen: bloating, poor appetite, loose stools, and a heavy, foggy feeling after eating.思慮過度——中醫認為思慮會「結」脾：腹脹、食慾差、大便溏稀、飯後昏沉沉重。
  • Long-standing stress — over months or years, constant tension can wear down the body’s reserves, leaving you tired yet wired, with poor sleep and low resilience.長期壓力——數月甚至數年持續緊張，會慢慢耗損身體的儲備，讓人又累又亢奮、睡不好、恢復力變差。

These are general descriptions from TCM theory, not a diagnosis. Your own pattern is identified in person — through conversation, tongue inspection and pulse — and it can change over time.以上只是中醫理論的一般描述，不是診斷。個人的證型需經面診——透過問診、看舌苔與把脈——才能判斷，而且證型會隨時間變化。

Gentle self-care for stress紓解壓力的日常調養

These are general habits, not a prescription. Try one or two at a time:以下是一般的生活習慣，不是處方。一次試一兩項就好：

1. Move every day1. 每天活動

Brisk walking, stretching, tai chi — movement is the classic way to “course” stuck Liver Qi. Stagnation hates movement, so even a 15-minute walk counts.快走、伸展、太極——活動是疏通鬱結肝氣的經典方法。鬱結最怕活動，即使散步 15 分鐘也有幫助。

2. Slow your breathing2. 放慢呼吸

When tension rises, try breathing in for 4 counts and out for 6, for a few minutes. Slow diaphragmatic breathing before bed can also help the mind settle.感到緊張時，試試吸氣 4 拍、呼氣 6 拍，持續幾分鐘。睡前做幾分鐘腹式慢呼吸，也有助心神安定。

3. Eat regularly, sleep regularly3. 定時吃飯、定時睡覺

Regular meals at regular times; limit alcohol and late caffeine; do not skip meals. Protect your sleep — being in bed by 11 p.m. when possible is a classic TCM recommendation.三餐定時定量；限制酒精與太晚的咖啡因；不要不吃飯。保護睡眠——晚上 11 點前上床，是中醫經典的建議。

4. Name the stressor4. 說出壓力來源

Unspoken frustration is the classic root of stuck Qi. Talking with someone you trust, journaling, or counselling genuinely counts as part of care.說不出口的煩惱，是氣鬱結的經典根源。找信任的人傾訴、寫日記或尋求諮詢，確實是調理的一部分。

5. Acupressure for tension5. 紓解緊張的穴位按壓

Press gently — enough to feel a mild ache or warmth, never pain. Stop if it feels uncomfortable.輕柔按壓——以感覺輕微痠脹或溫熱為度，不要按到痛。如果不舒服就停下來。

  • Neiguan 內關 (PC6) — three finger-widths above the wrist crease, between the two tendons. Press 2 minutes on each side; it also settles nausea and a tight chest.內關 (PC6)——手腕橫紋上方三指寬、兩條肌腱之間。每側按 2 分鐘；也能緩解噁心與胸悶。
  • Taichong 太沖 (LR3) — on top of the foot, in the valley between the big toe and second toe bones. Press firmly 2 minutes on each side — the classic “valve” for stuck Liver Qi.太沖 (LR3)——腳背上、大腳趾與第二趾骨之間的凹陷處。每側用力按 2 分鐘，是疏通鬱結肝氣的經典穴位。
  • Hegu 合谷 (LI4) — in the web between the thumb and index finger. Press 1–2 minutes on each side. Avoid strong stimulation in pregnancy.合谷 (LI4)——拇指與食指之間的虎口處。每側按 1 至 2 分鐘。懷孕期間請避免強刺激。

When to seek help promptly什麼情況要盡快求助

Self-care and acupuncture can support you, but some situations need prompt medical or mental-health care. Please reach out right away if you notice any of these:自我調養與針灸可以幫助您，但有些情況需要盡快尋求醫療或心理健康協助。如果出現以下任何情況，請立即求助：

  • Panic attacks, or anxiety that interferes with work, relationships or daily life for more than a couple of weeks驚恐發作，或焦慮持續數週以上，影響工作、人際關係或日常生活
  • Low mood most days, loss of interest in things you used to enjoy, or feeling hopeless多數日子情緒低落、對以往喜歡的事失去興趣，或感到絕望
  • Thoughts of harming yourself — please reach out right away. In Canada you can call or text 988, or dial 911 in an emergency.有傷害自己的念頭——請立即求助。在加拿大可撥打或傳訊 988，緊急情況請撥 911。
  • Chest pain, palpitations with dizziness or fainting, or severe shortness of breath — seek urgent medical care胸痛、心悸伴隨頭暈或昏厥，或嚴重呼吸困難——請立即就醫
