Neck & shoulder pain頸肩疼痛

Neck and shoulder pain is one of the most common reasons people seek acupuncture. Hours at a desk, looking down at a phone, sleeping in an awkward position, stress, or a cold draft can all leave the neck stiff, the shoulders tight, and turning the head painful. Sometimes the pain stays local; sometimes it spreads into the arms or up into the head. Occasional stiffness is common. When pain lasts for weeks, keeps coming back, or starts to limit what you can do, it is worth paying attention to.頸肩疼痛是人們尋求針灸最常見的原因之一。長時間伏案工作、低頭看手機、睡姿不當、壓力大，或吹了冷風，都可能讓脖子僵硬、肩膀緊繃、轉頭疼痛。有時疼痛只在局部，有時會蔓延到手臂或頭部。偶爾僵硬很常見；如果疼痛持續數週、反覆發作，或開始影響日常活動，就值得留意。

How TCM looks at neck & shoulder pain中醫怎麼看頸肩疼痛

In TCM, neck and shoulder pain is not one single thing. A practitioner asks how the pain behaves, because different patterns point in different directions. A few patterns often described in TCM texts:在中醫看來，頸肩疼痛不是單一的問題。醫師會問疼痛是「怎麼」表現的，因為不同的表現指向不同的證型。以下是中醫文獻中常提到的幾種情況：

  • Stiffness and aching that feel worse with cold or stress — often discussed as Qi and Blood not flowing smoothly through the channels of the neck and shoulders. The area may feel tight, heavy, and hard to move.僵硬痠痛，遇冷或壓力大時加重——常與頸肩經絡氣血運行不暢有關；患處感覺緊繃、沉重、活動困難。
  • Sudden pain after a cold draft or sleeping awkwardly — often described as Wind-Cold settling in the neck. The pain can come on overnight and make turning the head difficult.吹冷風或睡姿不當後突然疼痛——常以風寒侵襲頸部來描述；疼痛可能一夜之間出現，轉頭困難。
  • Tension that builds with stress and poor posture — the shoulders creep up toward the ears, the upper back tightens, and headaches may follow. In TCM this is often discussed as Liver Qi not flowing smoothly.壓力大、姿勢不良時緊張加重——肩膀不自覺聳起、上背緊繃，有時伴隨頭痛。中醫常以肝氣不暢來描述。
  • Aching that comes with age or overuse — a dull, persistent ache that is worse after activity and better with rest and warmth. In TCM this is often discussed in terms of the Kidney and the nourishment of sinews and bones.隨年齡或過度使用而出現的痠痛——隱隱作痛、活動後加重、休息和保暖後緩解。中醫常從腎與筋骨的濡養來理解。

These are general descriptions from TCM theory, not a diagnosis. Your own pattern is identified in person — through conversation, tongue inspection and pulse — and it can change over time.以上只是中醫理論的一般描述，不是診斷。個人的證型需經面診——透過問診、看舌苔與把脈——才能判斷，而且證型會隨時間變化。

Gentle self-care for neck & shoulders頸肩日常調養

These are general habits, not a prescription. Try one or two at a time:以下是一般的生活習慣，不是處方。一次試一兩項就好：

1. Warmth1. 保暖

Keep the neck warm, especially in cold or windy weather — a scarf helps. A warm compress or warm shower on the tight area for 10–15 minutes can help the muscles relax.注意頸部保暖，尤其在寒冷或有風的天氣——圍一條圍巾有幫助。用熱毛巾熱敷緊繃處 10 至 15 分鐘，或洗個熱水澡，有助肌肉放鬆。

2. Move gently2. 輕柔活動

Move the neck slowly — gentle turns, tilts, and shoulder rolls, a few times a day. Move only within a comfortable range, and stop if pain is sharp.慢慢活動脖子——輕柔地轉頭、側傾、聳肩，每天幾次。只在舒服的範圍內活動，如果出現劇痛就停下來。

3. Posture3. 姿勢

Raise screens to eye level, take a short break from sitting every 30–60 minutes, and avoid sleeping on a pillow that is too high or too flat.把螢幕抬到與視線平齊，每坐 30 至 60 分鐘起來活動一下，避免枕頭過高或過低。

4. Acupressure4. 穴位按壓

Press gently — enough to feel a mild ache or warmth, never pain. Stop if it feels uncomfortable.輕柔按壓——以感覺輕微痠脹或溫熱為度，不要按到痛。如果不舒服就停下來。

  • Jianjing 肩井 (GB21) — on top of the shoulder, midway between the neck and the tip of the shoulder. Press gently 1–2 minutes on each side. Avoid in pregnancy.肩井 (GB21)——肩膀頂部、脖子與肩峰之間的中點。每側輕按 1 至 2 分鐘。懷孕期間請避免使用。
  • Fengchi 風池 (GB20) — in the hollows at the base of the skull, on either side of the neck. Press gently 1–2 minutes. Often used for neck stiffness and tension headaches.風池 (GB20)——頭骨底部兩側、脖子旁的凹陷處。輕按 1 至 2 分鐘。常用於頸部僵硬與緊張性頭痛。
  • Hegu 合谷 (LI4) — in the web between the thumb and index finger. Press 1–2 minutes on each hand. Avoid in pregnancy.合谷 (LI4)——拇指與食指之間的虎口處。每隻手按 1 至 2 分鐘。懷孕期間請避免使用。

When to seek prompt medical care什麼情況要盡快看醫生

Most neck and shoulder pain settles with time and gentle care, but some signs need prompt medical assessment. Please see your family doctor or urgent care promptly if you notice any of these:大多數頸肩疼痛會隨著時間和細心調養慢慢好轉，但有些徵兆需要盡快做醫療評估。如果出現以下任何情況，請盡快看家庭醫生或急診：

  • Numbness, tingling or weakness in the arms or hands手臂或手麻木、刺痛或無力
  • Pain after a fall, accident, or sudden injury跌倒、意外或突然受傷後出現疼痛
  • Fever with a stiff neck, or a severe headache that is new發燒伴隨脖子僵硬，或新出現的嚴重頭痛
  • Dizziness, difficulty swallowing or speaking, or changes in vision頭暈、吞嚥或說話困難，或視力變化
  • Pain that does not improve after a few weeks, or keeps getting worse幾週後仍無改善，或疼痛持續加重
  • Chest pain or shortness of breath — seek urgent medical care胸痛或呼吸困難——請立即就醫
