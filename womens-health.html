Women's health婦女健康

Women's health changes across a lifetime — first periods, the years of menstruation, pregnancy, and perimenopause. In TCM, the menstrual cycle is read as a monthly report on the body: its regularity, flow, pain, and the mood and energy around it all give information about Blood, Qi, and the Liver and Kidney systems. When the cycle is steady and comfortable, it is a sign the body is in balance. When it is not, TCM looks for the pattern behind it.婦女健康在一生中不斷變化——初潮、月經期、懷孕、圍絕經期。在中醫看來，月經週期是身體每月一次的報告：是否規律、經量多少、有無疼痛，以及前後的情緒與精神狀態，都反映了血、氣與肝、腎的狀況。月經規律舒適，是身體平衡的表現；如果不規律，中醫會尋找背後的證型。

How TCM looks at women's health中醫怎麼看婦女健康

In TCM, women's health centres on Blood and the Liver and Kidney systems. A practitioner asks about the cycle in detail, because different patterns point in different directions:在中醫看來，婦女健康的核心是血，以及肝、腎系統。醫師會詳細詢問月經週期的情況，因為不同的表現指向不同的證型：

  • Scanty, light periods with fatigue and dizziness — often discussed as Blood not being plentiful enough to nourish the body. Periods may be late, light, or pale.經量少、顏色淡，伴隨疲倦、頭暈——常與血不足以濡養身體有關；月經可能推遲、量少、色淡。
  • Period pain, irritability and breast tenderness before the period — often discussed as Liver Qi not flowing smoothly. Pain that eases once flow starts is a classic sign.經前疼痛、煩躁、乳房脹痛——常與肝氣不暢有關；月經來了之後疼痛緩解是典型表現。
  • Cycle changes with age — perimenopause — hot flashes, night sweats, broken sleep and mood changes. In TCM this is often discussed as Kidney Yin declining with age, sometimes with Liver Qi involved.隨年齡變化的週期——圍絕經期——潮熱、盜汗、睡眠中斷、情緒波動。中醫常以腎陰隨年齡漸衰來描述，有時與肝氣有關。
  • Heavy periods with bloating and a heavy feeling — often described as Dampness and Phlegm weighing the body down.經量多、腹脹、身體沉重——常以痰濕困重來描述。

These are general descriptions from TCM theory, not a diagnosis. Your own pattern is identified in person — through conversation, tongue inspection and pulse — and it can change over time.以上只是中醫理論的一般描述，不是診斷。個人的證型需經面診——透過問診、看舌苔與把脈——才能判斷，而且證型會隨時間變化。

Period pain & irregular cycles經痛與月經不規律

Painful periods are common, but pain that regularly keeps you from work, school or sleep deserves attention. The same goes for cycles that are very irregular, very short or very long, or periods that stop altogether. Keeping a simple note of your cycle — dates, flow, pain, mood and sleep — gives a practitioner useful information at a first visit.經痛很常見，但如果疼痛經常讓您無法工作、上學或睡覺，就值得留意。週期非常不規律、過短或過長，或月經停止，也一樣值得留意。簡單記錄月經週期——日期、經量、疼痛、情緒與睡眠——初診時能給醫師提供有用的信息。

Warmth保暖

Keep the lower abdomen warm — a hot water bottle, warm drinks, and avoiding cold foods and cold exposure during the period help many people.注意小腹保暖——熱水袋、溫熱飲品，經期避免生冷食物與受涼，對很多人有幫助。

Rest and gentle movement休息與輕柔活動

Rest more on heavy days. Gentle walking or stretching is fine; intense exercise can wait until bleeding eases.經量多的日子多休息。散步或輕柔伸展都可以；劇烈運動可以等經血減少後再做。

Acupressure穴位按壓

Press gently — enough to feel a mild ache or warmth, never pain. Stop if it feels uncomfortable.輕柔按壓——以感覺輕微痠脹或溫熱為度，不要按到痛。如果不舒服就停下來。

  • Sanyinjiao 三陰交 (SP6) — four finger-widths above the inner ankle bone, just behind the shin bone. Press 2 minutes on each side. A classic point for menstrual health. Avoid strong stimulation in pregnancy.三陰交 (SP6)——內踝骨上方四指寬、脛骨後緣處。每側按 2 分鐘，是調理月經的經典穴位。懷孕期間請避免強刺激。
  • Taichong 太衝 (LV3) — on top of the foot, in the hollow between the big toe and the second toe. Press gently 1–2 minutes on each side. Often used for irritability and tension before the period.太衝 (LV3)——腳背上、大腳趾與第二趾之間的凹陷處。每側輕按 1 至 2 分鐘。常用於經前煩躁與緊張。

Perimenopause & menopause圍絕經期與更年期

Perimenopause can last several years, and symptoms vary widely: hot flashes, night sweats, broken sleep, mood changes, and vaginal dryness are common. In TCM, care focuses on the pattern behind the symptoms — often Kidney Yin — with acupuncture, rest, and daily habits. Acupuncture is support, not a replacement for medical care: it is worth discussing all options, including hormone therapy, with your family doctor.圍絕經期可能持續數年，症狀因人而異：潮熱、盜汗、睡眠中斷、情緒波動、陰道乾澀都很常見。中醫調理著重症狀背後的證型——常與腎陰有關——配合針灸、休息與日常習慣。針灸是輔助支持，不能替代醫療：包括荷爾蒙治療在內的各種選擇，都值得和家庭醫生討論。

Daily habits日常習慣

Keep regular sleep hours, dress in layers you can remove, keep the bedroom cool at night, and try gentle exercise such as walking or tai chi. Spicy food, alcohol and caffeine can trigger hot flashes for some people.保持規律作息，穿方便穿脫的層疊衣物，晚上保持臥室涼爽，試試散步或太極這類輕柔運動。辛辣食物、酒精與咖啡因可能誘發某些人的潮熱。

Fertility & trying to conceive助孕與備孕

If you are trying to conceive, TCM support looks at the whole picture — cycle regularity, flow, pain, sleep, stress and general health — alongside any medical care you are already receiving. For more detail, see our fertility support page. When to seek medical care什麼情況要看醫生

TCM support works best alongside proper medical care. Please see your family doctor promptly if you notice any of these:中醫調理最好與正規醫療配合。如果出現以下任何情況，請盡快看家庭醫生：

  • Very heavy bleeding — soaking through pads every hour, or large clots經血非常多——每小時浸透衛生棉，或有大血塊
  • Severe pelvic pain, or pain that is new and different嚴重的骨盆疼痛，或新出現的不同性質的疼痛
  • Bleeding between periods, or any bleeding after menopause非經期出血，或停經後任何出血
  • Fever, unusual discharge, or foul-smelling discharge發燒、異常分泌物，或分泌物有異味
  • Irregular or absent periods, or very painful periods月經不規律或停經，或經痛非常嚴重
  • If you are over 35 and have been trying for more than 6 months, or over 40, seek medical advice sooner35 歲以上、嘗試超過 6 個月仍未懷孕，或 40 歲以上，請提早尋求醫療建議
